# Technical Reference Specification - Cloud Platform v4.2

## 1. Availability & Disaster Recovery SLA
Target monthly platform availability is 99.5% excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
Disaster recovery replication operates asynchronously with a Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 4 hours.

## 2. Security & Cryptography Standards
All persistent volumes use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 for all external and internal traffic.
Data centers maintain active ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II compliance certifications.

## 3. Support Operations & Implementation Timeline
Standard enterprise support operates during business hours (Monday to Friday, 09:00-18:00 UTC) with a 4-hour P1 response target.
Standard onboarding and production migration require 12 weeks from kickoff to go-live.
